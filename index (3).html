About us

SP PCB Design is a small electronics workshop in Noida, Uttar Pradesh. We have spent fourteen years designing circuit boards, repairing equipment and supplying components — which means the person who designs your board is the same person who will pick up the phone when something doesn't work.

We take on jobs of any size, from one repaired board to a batch of prototypes, and we are happy to talk through a design before you spend anything.

  • Industrial and manufacturing units
  • Research and testing laboratories
  • Startups building first hardware
  • Engineering students and colleges
  • Repair and service workshops
